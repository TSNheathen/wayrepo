# Akros data-authority and integration prerequisites

> **Historical evidence notice (superseded 2026-08-26):** This research captured the decision frame before the OntOS composition and provider-boundary corrections. Its unconstrained optional-Symmy/direct-Connector alternatives are not current architecture. Symmy is now the preferred, non-exclusive hub for business-system integrations it provides; owner-local Direct Provider Adapters are allowed only for provider families outside Symmy or a required route Symmy does not supply. Use the [corrected integration decision](../product/akros-data-authority-integration-contracts.md).

Research for **[Define Akros data authority and integration contracts](https://github.com/TSNheathen/wayrepo/issues/8)**.

This note stays at business-roadmap depth. It identifies what current evidence can support in the authority decision and, equally importantly, what still needs an accountable owner confirmation. The ticket prepares the **Akros side** of each contract. It does not require discovery of the systems behind Symmy and does not select field schemas, endpoints, schedules, deployment topology, or provider implementations.

## Evidence rule

A **System of Record** is authorized per business fact or lifecycle transition, not per application. A Connector can transport or transform a fact without owning it, and an Integration Hub is not automatically authoritative (`CONTEXT.md:21-39`). The Production Deployment Snapshot proves deployed seams, not their enablement or activity (`CONTEXT.md:41-54`).

The accepted routing boundary is that **Symmy is an optional, replaceable Integration Hub**. Akros may exchange a fact through the commerce-side Symmy Connector, through a direct Connector, or through both where a deliberate coexistence rule requires it. This ticket defines the Akros-owned authority and contract expectations at that boundary; Symmy is responsible for its own downstream coordination.

The evidence therefore separates:

- **confirmed current use**: runtime observation or explicit operator confirmation;
- **implemented or deployed seam**: a credible contract candidate, not proof of current Akros use;
- **target-domain responsibility**: an accepted Akros/OntOS boundary, not proof of which runtime system supplies each source fact.

## Authority clues by business fact

The decision can assign the target Akros-side authority and the contract expected at its boundary without discovering Symmy's downstream topology.

## Decision prerequisites still missing

Issue 8 can set Akros-side principles now, but it cannot truthfully assign authority and contract behavior until the following owner confirmations exist:

1. **Akros Integration owner and Akros Operations:** for each material fact family, choose the Akros boundary as direct Connector, optional Symmy Connector, or deliberate coexistence, and state the broad direction. Neither Symmy's downstream systems nor a complete provider inventory is a prerequisite.
2. **Catalog/B2B owner:** assign target Akros-side authority and intentional local overrides for Product identity, description/content, classification, assortment, price, tax inputs, stock, Availability, Commerce-owned customer profiles/workflows, and external identifiers; Party/Counterparty identity authority remains fixed by [Define Party Registry, CRM Customer, and commerce Counterparty ownership](https://github.com/TechsioCZ/ontos/issues/95).
3. **Commerce Operations plus the Akros Integration owner:** define the Akros-side Connector capability required per fact family and classify the expected cadence as live, periodic, or manual where this changes coexistence or recovery. Selecting a provider-specific path can follow later.
4. **Finance/Tax and Payment owner:** assign Akros-side Payment and financial-document transition authority, reconciliation responsibility, statutory preservation boundary, and treatment of open obligations; name providers only when a direct Akros Connector makes that necessary.
5. **Fulfillment owner:** assign the Akros-side Fulfillment lifecycle, external outcomes accepted at the boundary, and treatment of open shipments, returns, and exceptions; carrier/WMS selection can remain provider-neutral.
6. **Content/SEO/Acquisition/Privacy and Communications owners:** assign Akros-side Content authority and the outbound/inbound outcomes needed for feeds, measurement, email, consent, and externally visible continuity; provider discovery is not required here.
7. **Core/platform and owning-module owners:** prove Core effect deduplication and request-conflict behavior, then assign any required semantic response reconstruction or bounded acknowledgement replay to the owning Business Module or Connector; close stuck-work reclamation, durable callback delivery, and commerce-grade reconciliation gaps before those capabilities become launch dependencies (`docs/research/ontos-erp-commerce-assessment.md:85-94`; [OntOS #97](https://github.com/TechsioCZ/ontos/issues/97)).
8. **Data/migration owner:** name the retained mapping/history classes and provide aggregate open-record/mapping counts; current production-derived files prove material identity/mapping scale but not live counts (`docs/research/akros-runtime-business-facts.md:29-35`).

For every unanswered Akros-side row, record a named person and follow-up date rather than assigning authority by inference. Existing evidence already rules out three shortcuts: **Symmy is not a universal System of Record, ABRA is not an assumed active ERP, and `new-engine` is not an already-running Akros replacement.**

## Decision-ready minimum

At roadmap level, each required fact family or transition needs one concise row containing:

- canonical fact or lifecycle transition;
- System of Record and accountable business owner;
- permitted Akros-local override and conflict rule;
- Akros-side producer/consumer role and routing through a direct Connector, optional Symmy Connector, or both;
- surviving Akros-side Connector or an explicit migration disposition;
- broad trigger/cadence and freshness expectation;
- business idempotency key and duplicate outcome;
- retry/replay/backfill/reconciliation/partial-failure/manual-recovery rule and owner;
- audit/retention obligation; and
- coexistence, cutover, rollback, and open-obligation consequence.

That is sufficient for the Wayfinder decision. Field mappings, schemas, endpoint definitions, exact backoff, queue selection, and runbook commands belong in the later specification.

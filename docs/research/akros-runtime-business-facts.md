Research asset for **[Capture runtime facts needed for integration and migration](https://github.com/TSNheathen/wayrepo/issues/5)**.

This note stays at business-roadmap depth. It applies the canonical OntOS/Akros terms **External Business System**, **System of Record**, and **Connector** without deciding technical contracts. All local paths are relative to the `wayrepo` root. No secrets, personal data, raw payloads, or row-level records are reproduced; controlled personal-data artifacts were measured only by line count.

## Evidence boundary

The operator-confirmed Production Deployment Snapshot proves deployed Akros code, routes, customizations, and Connector seams, but not database-controlled enablement, traffic, schedules, or operator use (`CONTEXT.md:33-42`). It does not contain the live database, runtime activity, or accountable ownership. Consequently, no operational External Business System can be declared active solely from this snapshot. Current public observations below establish only what the public Akros surfaces served on 2026-08-22.

## Scale, history, and continuity facts

- Controlled production-derived artifacts establish approximate identity/migration scale: 28,398 customer/external-mapping rows, 21,634 historical identity/email rows, 7,306 cleanup rows, and 23,789 price-type update rows. These were verified by line count only; their contents were not inspected. They prove material identity volume and quality risk, not current customer count or ABRA activity (controlled sources: `../context/team-1633516729428-akros-124b361ce570/project/customer.csv`, `emaily.txt`, `report.txt`, and `test.log`).
- The accepted launch cutline requires continuity for active Retail Customer identities and Principal bindings, approved Counterparty relationships and permissions, required addresses/consents, open orders, unresolved payments/shipments, active approval baskets, open claims, and mappings needed to continue them. Current counts are unavailable (`docs/product/akros-product-cutline.md:55-58`).
- Historical orders, invoices/credit documents, and claims require an accountable retention decision; exact periods are not evidenced (`docs/product/akros-product-cutline.md:117-121`).
- Both [B2C](https://www.akros.cz/) and [B2B](https://b2b.akros.cz/) were publicly reachable on 2026-08-22. Akros also serves crawl-control and sitemap routing and rewrites product/content URLs (`../context/team-1633516729428-akros-124b361ce570/public_html/.htaccess:74-92`; [current robots policy](https://www.akros.cz/robots.txt)). URL, redirect, sitemap, structured-data, and active-feed continuity are therefore material cutover concerns, while current indexed scale and high-value URL classes remain unknown (`docs/product/akros-product-cutline.md:25-31,88-90`).
